# Skill: Forex research

**Purpose:** turn market observation into falsifiable, testable hypotheses.

**Inputs:** price data, (Phase 2+) economic calendar, central bank decisions, rates, sentiment.

**Procedure**
1. Observe: describe what happened (trend, range, volatility, session behaviour, news reaction).
2. Hypothesise in one sentence that could be proven false, naming pair, timeframe, condition
   and expected effect. Example: "On EURUSD H1, breakouts of the 72-bar range during the
   London session continue at least 1.5 ATR more often than not."
3. Check the experiment log first (`forexlab trials`) and say how many configs already exist.
   A hypothesis that needs a large grid to work is probably noise.
4. Hand off to `strategy_development.md`.

**Must not:** claim an edge from news reading or charts alone (constitution rule 8).
